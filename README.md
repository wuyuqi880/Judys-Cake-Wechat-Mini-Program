# JUDY’S CAKE
### WeChat Mini Program | Cake Ordering & Business Management

**A mobile-first digital experience designed for a real bakery business.**

JUDY’S CAKE is a WeChat Mini Program project that brings together cake discovery, personalized ordering experiences, reservation workflows, and business management within a unified mobile platform.

The project focuses on transforming everyday bakery operations into structured digital workflows while delivering an intuitive and visually refined customer experience.

---

## Project Overview

Traditional bakery businesses often manage product inquiries, customization requests, reservations, and customer orders through manual communication.

JUDY’S CAKE explores a more integrated approach by combining customer-facing interactions with operational management tools in a single WeChat ecosystem.

The platform is designed around two complementary experiences:

**Customer Experience**

An intuitive mobile interface for browsing cake collections, exploring product details, selecting customization options, and navigating reservation and ordering workflows.

**Business Management**

A mobile-oriented management interface designed to support product information, administrative access, reservation coordination, and order operations.

## Key Features & Design Areas

**Product Discovery & Catalog**

- Categorized cake collections and product exploration
- Image-focused product cards and detailed galleries
- Product specifications and customization options
- Mobile-friendly navigation and browsing interactions

**Reservation & Ordering Experience**

- Cake size, flavor, and quantity selection
- Personalized cake messages and special requests
- Pickup and delivery workflow design
- Reservation dates and time-slot selection
- Shopping cart and order management interfaces

**Business Administration**

- Role-based management architecture
- Product catalog and content management
- Reservation and order workflow modeling
- Administrative access and permission design
- Shared business data and operational coordination

### Engineering Considerations

Beyond the user interface, the project explores several practical software engineering challenges:

- Designing a unified experience for customers and administrators
- Translating bakery operations into structured application workflows
- Managing product variants and customization requirements
- Modeling appointment availability and reservation constraints
- Designing consistent order states and transitions
- Considering authentication, authorization, and transaction integrity
- Keeping the application modular and maintainable
